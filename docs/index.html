Both cartridges, the MSX one and the MSX2 one, in a single listing: the six pyramids with their 60 stages and every screen, Vick with his six tools, the four enemies, the stage editor, the two secret passwords, the two hidden minigames and the music taken from its own engine.

There are no screenshots here. Every image is drawn by running the cartridge's own code (the Z80, the mapper, the BIOS and the VDP of each machine) and checked against openMSX: stage 1, identical byte for byte in VRAM; the demos, with identical sprite tables for 1,200 frames.

What is inside

The game

  • The pyramids: the six pyramids and their 60 stages, every map whole in both editions.
  • The screens: the 154 screens, one by one, on the MSX and on the MSX2.
  • Vick: the hero with each tool and everything there is in the stages.
  • The enemies: the four enemies, with their names from the editor.
  • The sequences: the intro, the demos, the menu, the password, the editor, the cleared pyramid and the ending.
  • The music: the 16 tunes, from its own sound engine.
  • The texts: every text in the cartridge.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the passwords FESTIVAL and TRYAGAIN, the hidden spots, the editor and where it saves, what changes from MSX to MSX2, what is still open.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding both ROMs, the mapper, the interrupt, the RAM and the code.
